# Domain glossary

## Post

A dated piece of writing with a title, ordered categories, body, and optional summary. Its ordered categories and filename determine its public URL.

## Post URL

The permanent public location of a Post. It contains every ordered category followed by the filename slug and ends with a trailing slash.

## Legacy redirect

A historical public location that sends readers and crawlers to a Post URL while preventing the historical location from being indexed.

## Social image

The image and dimensions used when a page is shared. Posts use generated, fingerprinted images unless they explicitly provide an override.
